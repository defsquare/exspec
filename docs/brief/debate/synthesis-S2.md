# Synthesis S2

## Agreements

- **Recordings prove reproduction of concrete scenarios, not general business rules (A, B, C, E).** A green replay proves that one call sequence from one seed reproduces its recorded HTTP status, response body, and API read-backs (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Result; A, B, C Round 1; E Round 2). In the specification layer, `Rule` conditions classify scenarios and predict nothing: a wrong condition misclassifies a scenario without turning a replay red, and nothing checks a rule's `:says` prose (`dev/spike/conduit/rules.README.md`, opening; `docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 5, 10; A, B, C Round 1; E Round 2).
- **Recorded scenarios bypass `.spec`'s pre-state construction and computed-value bottlenecks (A, B, C, E).** On Conduit, four `.spec` extraction benches and $52 proved 9 of 19 operations, required 33 commits to the generator and grammar, and left 23 clauses `inexpressible` (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, opening, Result). One 13 min 42 s agent session produced 166 recorded scenarios across all 19 operations, turning all five `inexpressible` behaviours—password hash check, title slugification, pagination, filtered counts, and omitted update fields (after the M5 loop)—into green cases that killed their mutants without re-implementing target formulas (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 2, 4, Result; A, B, C Round 1; E Round 2).
- **API read-backs triggered by DB row diffs and equality-class noise tokens outperform flat snapshots (A, B, C, E).** Using database diffs strictly as a `SideEffect` detector to schedule pre- and post-call HTTP `Projection` reads—plus reading back resources named in write calls even when no row changed—and encoding noisy values as intra-scenario equality tokens (`[:spike/noise 1]`) raised Conduit mutant kills from 17/24 to 22/24 without coupling verdicts to database schemas (`dev/spike/metamodel.edn`, path "detect"; `docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 6–8; A, C Round 1; E Round 2; A, B, C Round 3). All four participants retain these mechanisms in a rebuild (A Round 1; E Round 2; B, C Round 3).
- **First-match rule evaluation misfits non-alternative fields (A, B, C, E).** Evaluating rules as an ordered `cond` where the first matching condition wins fails on independent update fields: on Conduit, `PUT /user › image` had 0 classed scenarios because both scenarios sending `image` also sent `bio` or `username` (`dev/spike/conduit/rules.README.md`, opening; `docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 11; A, B, C Round 1; E Round 2). All four drop first-match as the sole structure for overlapping rules (A, B, C Round 1; E Round 2).
- **Boundary mutations remain untested in the spike (A, B, C, E).** All 95 agent-written rule-mutants on Conduit inverted or removed checks; none shifted a numeric or date boundary (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 11; A, B Round 1; E Round 2; C Round 3).
- **Target clock control and non-database state restoration are required and unsolved across both approaches (A, B, C, E).** On Fineract, same-day replays yielded 48–49 green of 52 (with 3–4 scenarios failing on nondeterministic transaction ordering), D+1 yielded 20 reds, and D+35 yielded 32 reds (`dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, Replays, Verdict). `.spec`'s `--now` replays only the generator's draw, not the target's clock (`docs/dsl.md`, `now`; A Round 1, 3; E Round 2; C Round 3). Database-only restore misses Fineract's JVM-resident ID pool, and restricting observation from 280 tables to 6 `watched.edn` tables leaves writes outside those 6 unchecked (`dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, Recording; A, C Round 1; E Round 2).
- **Holding Fineract's clock at day D still catches an outward +7-day bound shift on existing scenarios (A, B, E).** Because 8 class-(c) Fineract scenarios store absolute "tomorrow" dates that are refused (400/403) at D, widening a future-date check to D+7 accepts them at D and turns their recordings red (`dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, D + 1, Remedy 2 tried; E Round 2; A, B Round 3, where B withdraws its Round 1 zero-kill prediction).
- **Observations capture bugs as green behaviour and lack an executable place for desired intent (A, B, C, E).** Conduit's 4 crashes returning bodiless 401s and Petclinic's null-date 500s, ID-overwriting POSTs (201), and ignored path `ownerId`s (204) are recorded as green references (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 5, 14, 15). Settling clock control and separating normative intent from recorded observation must come first in any rebuild (A, B, C Round 1; E Round 2).

## Disagreements

### 1. Repairing the generative `.spec` (A) vs. Leaving `.spec` for recorded scenarios (C, E)
- **A's position:** `.spec` states explicit `ensure` postconditions, derives Malli generators from `Named states`, and automatically tests deterministic boundary edges (`docs/dsl.md`, Named states, `ensure` phrases, `otherwise`; `docs/adr/README.md`, ADR-0030, ADR-0058). While generated runs sample rather than exhaust a domain, failures tie directly to a general claim rather than freezing one scenario's output; `.spec` also separates normative clauses from unjudged statements via `::` and `inexpressible` (`docs/dsl.md`, The HTTP surface, `inexpressible`; A Round 1, 3).
- **C and E's position:** Driving a target into Malli-drawn pre-states via API traces (`docs/adr/README.md`, ADR-0057, ADR-0068) and fixing every result field (ADR-0070) fails structurally on one-way hashes, slugification, and multi-filter pagination, costing $52 and 33 compiler/generator commits for 47% of Conduit's operations (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, opening, Frame, Result; E Round 2; C Round 3). Furthermore, `.spec` extractions already characterized target bugs rather than independent requirements (`docs/adr/README.md`, ADR-0094, ADR-0103; `docs/dsl.md`, `invalid ownerId -> 500`; E Round 2; B, C Round 3).
- **Settling experiment:** Freeze a repaired `.spec` implementation and the recorded-scenario implementation on identical Conduit and Fineract slices, with controlled target clocks and equal run budgets. Evaluate both against 30 blind, independently selected and equivalence-labeled boundary and relational mutants per target, counting `inexpressible` clauses as unkilled, and measure whether `.spec` kills ≥10 percentage points more mutants without more false reds and at what build cost (A Round 1, 3; E Round 2).

### 2. A checked state-machine model (B) vs. Recorded oracle plus provenance-tagged classifier (E)
- **B's position:** Without a transition relation (pre-state, event, post-state), invariants, and time in `StartingWorldState`, a specification cannot refute a wrong rule on unrecorded states (`dev/spike/metamodel.edn`, StartingWorldState, Rule; B Round 1, 3). Deleting Fineract's 8 "tomorrow" scenarios and re-recording makes a widened bound green; recording a target that always computed `due = disbursed + 2 months` makes that bug green; and swapping any of Conduit's 23 identical bodiless-401 scenarios stays green (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 2, 5; `dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, Can facts.edn say…; B Round 1, 3).
- **E's position:** B conflates stating a formula with detecting regressions (E Round 2). At held day D, Fineract records 0 masked business dates and 0 masked amounts, so mutating `dueDate` from +1 month to +2 months turns schedule readings red without re-implementing calendar math; date bounds are tested on both sides at D by paired "today" and "tomorrow" scenarios; and provenance labels (`verified`, `declared-only`, `refuted`, `guarded`, `unguarded`) state exactly what observations support (`dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, Masked leaves; `dev/spike/metamodel.edn`, path "supported"; E Round 2).
- **Settling experiments:**
  1. At held day D on Fineract's 52 scenarios, inject: (a) +7-day future-date bound widening, (b) `due = disbursed + 2 months`, and (c) inward bound narrowing that refuses "today" on `submit` and `approve` (testing E's prediction that missing edge scenarios, not the held clock, cause survival) (E Round 2; B Round 3).
  2. Replay Fineract at D+35 using date-offset relations to test whether all 24 class-(b) clock-derived scenarios pass while a `+2 months` mutant turns red without pinning the day (B Round 1; `dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, D + 35, What would make (b) and (c) hold).

### 3. Existing tools without a custom metamodel (C) vs. Retaining `rules.edn`, `facts.edn`, and `structures.edn` (E, A, B)
- **C's position:** Drop `metamodel.edn`, `rules.edn`, `facts.edn`, and `structures.edn` (C Round 1, 3). The custom condition language has already grown to 20 operators while leaving 5 rules `:inexpressible` on Conduit, 9 on Petclinic, and 15 of 48 (31.25%) on Fineract, dumping 14 Fineract scenarios into false `otherwise` buckets (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 10, 14, 20; `dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, The spec layer). Petclinic's 200-vs-204 discrepancy came from `openapi.yml`, which OpenAPI validators check natively alongside formats, bounds, decimals, and `[y m d]` dates that `structures.edn` lacks or refutes (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 14, 16, 18; C Round 3). Bytecode mutation (PIT) and JaCoCo guide scenario generation directly, as Journal 4's source-line prompt did for M5 (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 4; C Round 3).
- **E's position (supported on evidence by A and B):** On Conduit, JaCoCo reported 0 unreached lines across all services, DTOs, controllers, and validators, yet `classify` identified 3 genuine rules lacking scenarios (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 3, 10, 11; E Round 2; C Round 3). On Petclinic, confronting declared claims with recordings exposed 12 update endpoints returning 204 instead of 200 (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 18; E Round 2; A, B Round 3).
- **Settling experiments:**
  1. Run two equal-budget agent loops expanding Fineract's 52 scenarios at held day D: Arm 1 guided by JaCoCo, PIT bytecode survivors, and OpenAPI validation (no `rules.edn`/`facts.edn`/`structures.edn`); Arm 2 guided by `classify` (frozen at 20 operators) and `spike.structures`. Compare kill rates on 30 blind boundary/relational mutants (testing C's ≥10pp margin for Arm 1) and schema-discrepancy recall (E Round 2; C Round 3).
  2. Expand to a 25-operation slice of Fineract or KADAI and measure whether `rules.README.md` grows from 20 to ≥27 operators or leaves ≥25% of rules `:inexpressible` (C Round 1, 3; E Round 2).

## Claims to check

### Refuted by the fact-check (F)
- **Conduit `:inexpressible` causes (B Round 1):** B claimed all 5 `:inexpressible` rules required slug computation or hash checking; 2 require computed values and 3 require list quantifiers (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 10; F; B Round 3).
- **Fineract refuted structure claims (C Round 1):** C attributed all 17 refuted claims to dates and amounts; 15 are dates or amounts, 1 is `Instalment` lacking `period` on row 0, and 1 is undeclared `Loan.transactions` keys (`dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, The spec layer; F; C Round 3).
- **Operator count (C Round 1):** C stated 15 operators in `rules.README.md` ("Terms"); the file lists 6 terms and 20 operators (11 base, 4 added, 5 arithmetic) (`dev/spike/conduit/rules.README.md`, Terms; F; C Round 3).
- **Existing PIT runs (C Round 1):** C cited PIT mutation kill scores and survivors as existing spike artefacts; PIT was never run and appears once under future work (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Next; F; E Round 2; B, C Round 3).
- **Petclinic unordered pets (C Round 1):** C stated the scenario was deleted; it was replaced, though omitted from the reference (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 14; F; C Round 3).

### Claims made without dossier support or marked `[outside the dossier]`
- **A (Round 1, 3):** The predicted ≥10 percentage point mutant-kill advantage for repaired `.spec` over recorded scenarios.
- **B (Round 1):** That transition relations render widened guards and swapped 401s reachable as failed post-states; that state-machine invariants are where normative intent belongs; that computing slugs in a model constitutes a second implementation; and that hand-written state machines replicate source-code bugs.
- **C (Round 1, 3):** Capabilities attributed to external tools—golden-master/approval frameworks (ApprovalTests, JSONassert), consumer-driven contract testing, host-language test assertions, OpenAPI validators natively checking 200-vs-204 drift and `[y m d]`/decimal/format/enum schemas, PIT mutating compiled bytecode `<` to `<=` bounds, off-the-shelf tools lacking DB-diff-triggered GET probes and equality-class noise tokens, `.spec` requiring bcrypt/slug/SQL re-implementation in BNF, and C's ≥10pp / 27-operator numerical thresholds.

## Open questions

- **Executable intent and review granularity (Q3):** Where desired behaviour is stated and judged when it contradicts legacy behaviour (Petclinic's 4 bugs and 12 `204` responses; Conduit's 4 crashes), and how intentional changes avoid unreviewable 3,600-line re-record diffs (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 5, 14, 15, 18; A, B, C, E).
- **Opaque error equivalence (Q1):** How any black-box API oracle verifies that distinct refusal rules ("not author" vs. "unknown article", or 7 unguarded `anonymous` routes) were not swapped or broken when the target returns identical bodiless 401s with zero side effects (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 2, 5, 11; A, B, C, E).
- **Clock control and cross-day relations (Q4):** How the harness freezes or advances JVM and database clocks when a target exposes no business-date API (such as Petclinic's `LocalDate.now()` or Conduit's expiring JWTs and session mutant C1), and whether references will compare dates as offsets to `today` (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 5, 6, 20, Backlog 12; `dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, The target and its clocks, Verdict; A, B, C, E).
- **Large-schema leakage, non-DB state, and unordered collections (Q4, Q5):** How to detect writes outside Fineract's 6 `watched.edn` tables (of 280) or async/scheduler tables, reset in-memory JVM pools/caches between scenarios, and compare nondeterministic list orders as sets (`dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, Recording, What the shapes…; `docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Journal 5, 14; A, B, C, E).
- **Unprojected state and multiplexed operations (Q1, Q5):** How to judge blind-spot writes with no read projection (Petclinic's `users`/`roles` tables; refused `POST /users` and `POST /articles` calls), verify cross-schema rewrites, and model Fineract's `?command=` transitions, envelope-only write responses, and path-only relations (`docs/branches/spike-recorded-cases.md`, Frame, Journal 8, 14, 15; `dev/spike/fineract/clock.md`, What the shapes…).
